Privacy policy

This page explains what personal data may be handled when you contact Marksyte through the website.

Data controller

Marksyte is responsible for data received through this website.

Contact email: contact@marksyte.com

Data collected

The website does not store contact form submissions in a database. The discovery form prepares an email draft in your email client. If you send that email, Marksyte may receive the name, email address, company or role, and challenge description you choose to include.

If you consent to analytics, Google Analytics may process online identifiers and technical usage information such as visited pages, approximate location, device, browser, and interaction data.

Purpose and legal basis

Contact details are used to respond to enquiries, arrange discovery conversations, and manage a potential business relationship. The basis is your request to be contacted and Marksyte's legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Analytics data is used to understand and improve website performance. This optional processing is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through “Cookie settings”.

Retention

Enquiry emails are kept only for as long as needed to manage the conversation, follow up on the request, or meet basic administrative obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or limit the use of your personal data, object to its processing, or request a copy in a portable format by emailing contact@marksyte.com.

If you believe your data has not been handled lawfully, you can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, such as the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or the data protection authority of your country.

International transfers

If you accept analytics, Google may process analytics data on servers located outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Transfers to Google are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or equivalent safeguards.

Automated decision-making

Marksyte does not use automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Third parties

Email delivery and hosting providers may process technical data as part of normal service delivery. If analytics is accepted, Google processes analytics data as the service provider. External websites linked from Marksyte are governed by their own privacy policies.
