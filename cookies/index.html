Cookie policy

This page explains the current cookie use on marksyte.com.

Current cookie use

Marksyte does not currently set non-essential cookies for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

Technical data

The hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and timestamp, to deliver the website securely and reliably.

Future analytics

If Marksyte adds analytics or other tools that use cookies or similar identifiers, this page will be updated and consent controls will be added where required.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking technical cookies may affect some websites, although Marksyte currently does not rely on optional cookies for its core pages.
