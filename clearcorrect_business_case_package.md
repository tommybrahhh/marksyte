# ClearCorrect Business Case Package

Role: Global Practice Growth & Patient Marketing Manager  
Audience: Head of Growth in Sales and Marketing  
Presentation time: 30 minutes maximum  
Recommended title: ClearCorrect Practice Growth and Full-Funnel Accelerator

## Strategic Thesis

ClearCorrect has strong foundations: a differentiated clear aligner system, Straumann Group credibility, digital workflow tools, clinical education, and existing practice marketing resources. The growth opportunity is to connect those assets into a scalable global practice-growth operating model that helps providers generate patient demand, improve consultation conversion, and increase case volume.

The business case is not to create "more marketing assets." It is to build a measurable global-to-local growth program that changes behavior at the practice level and gives markets, sales teams, and clinical education teams a practical playbook for case growth.

## Business Case In One Sentence

Launch a 12-month ClearCorrect Practice Growth and Full-Funnel Accelerator: a global program that segments target practices, diagnoses funnel gaps, deploys modular patient marketing and conversion playbooks, enables sales and clinical education teams, and measures incremental case growth through pilot markets before global scale.

## Business Context

ClearCorrect operates in a market where patient behavior, digital dentistry, AI-enabled workflows, clear aligners, and GP adoption are changing how orthodontics is delivered. Straumann Group positions orthodontics as part of a shift toward integrated, patient-friendly, accessible solutions.

The company already has valuable building blocks:

- Provider-facing practice growth resources, including marketing kits, conversion kits, ClearCorrect Day guides, patient journey audits, testimonial guides, onboarding guides, social media resources, and patient-facing materials.
- Digital and clinical tools such as ClearPilot, Doctor Portal, Ortho Campus, Case Gallery, Clinic App, and support resources.
- Straumann Group scale and ambition, including 2025 revenue of CHF 2.6bn, 8.9% organic revenue growth, 7.3M smiles, and an ambition to reach 10M smiles annually.

The opportunity is to turn those assets into a disciplined growth system with clear ownership, adoption metrics, conversion metrics, and ROI logic.

## Business Problem

The challenge is likely not lack of brand assets. It is inconsistent execution across markets and practices.

Common practice-level friction points:

- Practices may not know how to create qualified patient demand for clear aligners.
- Leads may be generated but not followed up quickly or consistently.
- Front-desk and treatment coordinator teams may lack scripts, confidence, or process discipline.
- Dentists may need stronger clinical confidence and case-selection support to increase utilization.
- Local markets may duplicate assets instead of scaling proven global playbooks.
- Sales teams may be asked to drive growth without a simple diagnostic, enablement path, or KPI system.

This creates a gap between global strategy and practice-level case growth.

## Growth Opportunity

ClearCorrect can increase commercial performance by improving three connected levers:

1. Demand generation: more qualified patients entering the practice funnel.
2. Conversion optimization: more leads becoming consultations and more consultations becoming starts.
3. Provider utilization: more active providers using ClearCorrect with confidence and consistency.

The strategic move is to manage these levers as one full-funnel growth program rather than separate marketing, sales, and education initiatives.

## Recommended Program

Program name: ClearCorrect Practice Growth and Full-Funnel Accelerator

Program ambition:

Create a repeatable global growth model that helps selected practices grow ClearCorrect cases through patient demand, conversion discipline, and provider enablement.

Core design principles:

- Global core, local flex: one global playbook adapted by market.
- Practical over theoretical: tools that sales teams and clinics can use immediately.
- Segment-specific: different paths for new providers, growth providers, DSOs, and high-potential practices.
- Full-funnel: focus on lead generation, lead nurturing, consult conversion, treatment start, and repeat utilization.
- Measurable: track adoption, funnel performance, case growth, and ROI.

## Target Practice Segments

Segment 1: New or recently onboarded providers

Goal: help them move from training to first cases and repeat utilization.

Likely needs:

- Simple patient conversation scripts.
- Case selection support.
- First 10 cases pathway.
- Confidence-building clinical education.
- Basic in-practice materials and social content.

Segment 2: Activated growth providers

Goal: increase case volume among providers already using ClearCorrect but below potential.

Likely needs:

- Local patient demand campaigns.
- Funnel tracking.
- Consultation conversion support.
- Staff training.
- ClearCorrect Day activation.

Segment 3: High-volume or DSO partners

Goal: standardize scalable workflows across multiple locations.

Likely needs:

- Multi-site playbooks.
- Central-local campaign templates.
- Standardized reporting.
- Staff enablement and adoption dashboards.
- Clear escalation paths between sales, clinical education, and marketing.

## Program Architecture

The program has six modules.

Module 1: Practice Growth Diagnostic

Purpose: identify each practice's biggest growth bottleneck.

Inputs:

- Current ClearCorrect cases per month or quarter.
- Active provider status and historical utilization.
- Source of patient leads.
- Lead-to-consult rate.
- Consult-to-start rate.
- No-show rate.
- Follow-up process.
- Staff confidence.
- Clinical confidence and case selection.
- Usage of ClearCorrect tools and assets.

Output:

- Practice Growth Scorecard.
- 90-day action plan.
- Priority funnel gap.
- Recommended playbook.

Module 2: Patient Demand Toolkit

Purpose: help practices create qualified patient demand.

Assets:

- Social media templates.
- Paid social and search campaign templates.
- Landing page or booking page guidance.
- In-practice posters, flyers, and referral cards.
- Patient education content.
- Before-and-after and case story guidance.
- Google Business Profile recommendations.
- Local content calendar.

Module 3: ClearCorrect Day Playbook

Purpose: create an event-based activation that drives consults and treatment starts.

Assets:

- Event timeline.
- Patient invitation templates.
- Staff roles.
- Booking scripts.
- Reminder messages.
- In-practice patient journey.
- Consultation flow.
- Post-event follow-up sequence.
- Measurement dashboard.

Module 4: Consultation Conversion Toolkit

Purpose: improve conversion from interest to treatment start.

Assets:

- Front-desk and treatment coordinator scripts.
- Patient objection handling.
- Visual explanation of clear aligner treatment.
- Malocclusion education talk tracks.
- Financing conversation guidance where legally and locally appropriate.
- Follow-up cadence for undecided patients.
- Post-consultation plan.

Module 5: Sales and Clinical Education Enablement

Purpose: align field teams around one growth method.

Assets:

- Rep diagnostic checklist.
- Practice segmentation guide.
- Monthly business review template.
- CE training map by provider segment.
- Conversation guide for practice owners.
- Field objection handling.
- Adoption tracker.

Module 6: Growth Dashboard and Optimization Loop

Purpose: make the program measurable and scalable.

Dashboard layers:

- Program adoption.
- Funnel performance.
- Case growth.
- Market-level rollout.
- Practice-level action completion.
- Feedback from sales, CE, and practices.

## Pilot Design

Recommended pilot: 3 markets, 12 weeks, 45-60 practices total.

Pilot market mix:

- One mature market with strong infrastructure and competitive pressure.
- One growth market with high potential and developing provider adoption.
- One DSO or multi-site partner environment.

Alternative: choose one global pilot market and one local example for the interview case. Spain is a credible local example if you want to demonstrate practical market thinking.

Pilot selection criteria:

- Practices with measurable case history.
- Sales team willingness to participate.
- Clear local market owner.
- Ability to track leads, consults, starts, and case submissions.
- Mix of provider maturity levels.

Pilot structure:

Weeks 0-2: Baseline and design

- Select practices.
- Capture baseline cases, lead sources, conversion rates, and current asset usage.
- Run Practice Growth Diagnostic.
- Assign each practice a 90-day action plan.
- Train sales and clinical education teams.

Weeks 3-8: Activation

- Launch patient demand campaigns.
- Run ClearCorrect Day or equivalent activation.
- Deploy consultation conversion toolkit.
- Enable front-desk and treatment coordinator teams.
- Monitor lead follow-up speed, consult bookings, and treatment starts.

Weeks 9-12: Optimize and prove

- Review weekly funnel performance.
- Identify winning assets and weak points.
- Compare pilot practices to baseline and, where possible, matched control practices.
- Package learnings for global rollout.

## Local Pilot Example: Spain

Local objective:

Increase ClearCorrect case starts among selected growth practices in Spain by improving patient demand generation, consultation conversion, and practice team confidence.

Target cohort:

- 20 practices across Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, and one secondary city cluster.
- Mix of GP practices and ortho-focused practices.
- Baseline case volume available for at least the previous quarter.

Local insight:

Patients are interested in esthetic orthodontics but may be price-sensitive, comparison-shopping, and slow to commit. Practices may have leads from social media, website forms, WhatsApp, or referrals, but conversion depends heavily on fast follow-up, clear patient education, confidence in the consultation, and financing or value framing.

Local execution:

- Localized Spanish patient campaign templates.
- WhatsApp-compatible lead nurturing sequence, subject to local legal and consent rules.
- ClearCorrect Day event-in-a-box for selected clinics.
- Google Business Profile and local SEO checklist.
- Social content calendar for practices.
- In-clinic conversion materials adapted from the global conversion kit.
- Staff training on consult flow, objection handling, and follow-up.
- Clinical education support on case selection and first/next cases.

Local funnel:

Awareness -> Lead -> Consultation booked -> Consultation attended -> Treatment plan discussed -> Patient starts -> Referral/testimonial

Local KPI assumptions to validate:

- 70%+ of pilot practices actively using the toolkit by week 8.
- 80%+ of participating sales reps trained.
- 10-15% uplift in ClearCorrect case starts versus baseline or matched control.
- 15-20% improvement in consult-to-start conversion where baseline tracking exists.
- Lead follow-up speed under 24 hours for participating practices.

These are working assumptions for the business case, not guaranteed outcomes.

## ROI Logic

Do not overclaim revenue without internal data. Use a formula-based model in the interview.

Basic formula:

Incremental cases = pilot practices x baseline cases x uplift percentage

Incremental gross contribution proxy = incremental cases x net revenue per case

Program ROI = incremental gross contribution proxy minus program cost, divided by program cost

Example using assumptions:

- 50 pilot practices.
- Average baseline: 10 ClearCorrect cases per quarter per practice.
- Baseline total: 500 cases per quarter.
- Uplift assumption: 12%.
- Incremental cases: 60 per quarter.
- Revenue impact: 60 x internal net revenue per case.

The power of the model is that it shows commercial logic while allowing ClearCorrect to insert actual revenue and margin assumptions.

## Risks And Mitigation

Risk: Markets adapt assets too heavily and lose consistency.

Mitigation: Use a global core/local flex model with mandatory funnel logic, optional creative adaptation, and clear brand/legal guardrails.

Risk: Sales teams see the program as extra work.

Mitigation: Make the diagnostic simple, connect it to account growth, and provide a monthly business review template that helps reps have stronger commercial conversations.

Risk: Practices generate leads but fail to convert them.

Mitigation: Treat conversion as equal to demand generation. Include front-desk scripts, consultation flow, follow-up cadence, and practice staff training.

Risk: Results are hard to prove.

Mitigation: Capture baseline data, define control groups where possible, and measure adoption plus funnel metrics, not just final case volume.

Risk: Clinical confidence limits utilization.

Mitigation: Pair patient marketing with clinical education and case-selection support.
