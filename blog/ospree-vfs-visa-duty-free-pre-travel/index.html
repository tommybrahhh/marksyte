Ospree brings duty free into the visa application journey

A code delivered during a visa application connects an early signal of intent with a later airport purchase. The opportunity is meaningful, but it depends on incrementality, margin and experience.

Ospree Duty Free and VFS Global have joined two moments that are usually managed separately. Eligible UK visa applicants at participating centres in India receive a code for later use at duty-free stores in Mumbai and Ahmedabad.

In brief
  • The promotion launched on 29 June and provides a flat 10% discount, subject to terms and exclusions.
  • The partnership allows Ospree to engage travellers before they arrive at the airport.
  • No figures have been published for codes delivered, redemptions, visits or sales. Value needs to be demonstrated through the full funnel.

The key pointA visa application creates an early signal of travel intent. Turning it into commercial value requires permission, relevance, a simple experience and testing that separates incremental sales from the discount given.

What is happening

The partnership targets eligible customers applying for UK visas at participating VFS Global centres in India. Specialist reports say they receive unique codes providing a 10% discount at Ospree stores in Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport and Ahmedabad's Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport.

The offer covers beauty, wines and spirits, confectionery, electronics, lifestyle and gifting. Ospree describes it as part of a wider strategy to extend the customer relationship beyond the terminal. The reports say it can support departing and arriving passengers, subject to the applicable conditions.

The agreement does not turn the visa centre into a shop. It turns the moment into an acquisition touchpoint. The purchase still happens at the airport, while Mumbai also allows travellers to pre-book duty-free products and collect them during the journey.

Why the visa process is valuable

Intent appears before the final booking

When someone applies for a visa, a destination and travel purpose already exist. The final itinerary may not be complete, but the moment contains more context than a mass campaign: destination country, broad journey type and an approximate time window.

The retailer gains time to explain

Inside the terminal, the traveller faces security, boarding, food, messages and queues. Before travel, they can discover products, compare prices, save favourites and pre-order. This matters for considered categories such as beauty, premium drinks, electronics and gifts.

India is a material UK travel market

UK government data show that Indian nationals received 95,231 sponsored study visas in the year ending December 2025, the largest number by nationality. This is not the campaign's reach, but it shows India's importance within some UK travel flows.

VFS provides scale and repeated contact

VFS Global says it operates 4,180 centres in 168 countries for 71 client governments. Ospree's programme is specific and limited, but it demonstrates how a mobility intermediary can become an acquisition channel for retailers, brands and travel services.

What it means for tourism and travel retail

The case moves the boundary of travel retail. The commercial relationship no longer needs to begin at the terminal entrance. It can start during the visa, booking, insurance, currency or baggage-planning process.

For duty free, this creates time to build demand before competing for physical attention. A code can open a destination-led selection, an allowances guide, a pre-order service or a gifting recommendation. It becomes more useful when the content solves a real decision.

For brands, pre-travel context supports a more relevant proposition. A student, family visitor and business traveller may need different products and budgets. Personalisation should use consent and the minimum data required.

For airports, early acquisition can improve inventory planning and the distribution of visits. It also creates an opportunity to connect retail, food, transport, lounges and other services in one sequence.

Design the pre-travel journey

Risks and practical barriers

  • Confusing codes with customers. Delivery, opening, visit and purchase are separate stages.
  • Discounting sales that would have happened anyway. Without a comparison group, redemption may look like growth while only reducing margin.
  • Creating expectations before the itinerary is known. The applicant may not travel, may use another airport or may not meet the conditions.
  • Using too much data. Visa and mobility journeys require particularly careful governance.
  • Breaking the store experience. Exclusions, failed codes or uninformed staff destroy trust.
  • Scaling without consistency. Each destination, airport, allowance and category may require different rules.
Measurement note

The announced partnership does not include redemption or sales results. This article examines the commercial potential and the measures required to validate it.

Operational implications

Funnel design

Define eligibility, contact, engagement, visit, purchase and repeat behaviour.

Incrementality measurement

Compare groups, margin and behaviour to separate real effect from discount.

Product recommendation

Adapt categories to destination, mission, budget and travel moment.

Demand forecasting

Turn early signals into inventory, staffing and capacity by airport.

Incentive optimisation

Test discount, content, bundles, reservation or non-cash benefits.

AI and data governance

Minimise information, control access and audit recommendations.

AI can prioritise messages and products, but it should not infer sensitive information or use the visa process opaquely. Trust is part of the commercial result.

A practical 90-day agenda

  1. Define the hypothesis. State which behaviour should change and what margin should be created.
  2. Design the funnel. Record code, engagement, reservation, visit, sale and cost.
  3. Test two incentives. Compare discount with pre-order, a gift or useful content.
  4. Audit privacy and operations. Review consent, exclusions, POS and staff training.

Ospree and VFS Global show that duty free can enter the conversation before the airport. The value will not come from sending more coupons. It will come from using an early signal to provide a relevant experience and proving that it creates profitable growth.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use the Ospree and VFS Global offer?

Specialist media report that it is available to eligible applicants at participating VFS Global UK visa application centres in India. The code provides a 10% discount at Ospree stores in Mumbai and Ahmedabad, subject to terms and exclusions.

Can travellers shop during the visa application process?

No. The application is the acquisition and voucher-delivery touchpoint. The purchase takes place later at participating duty-free stores. The sources do not describe an integrated checkout inside the visa process.

How should performance be measured?

The funnel should track eligible applicants, codes delivered, opens, pre-orders, visits, purchases, margin and repeat behaviour. A comparable group without the incentive is needed to estimate incrementality.
