#### Design Specificity Verdict

The page feels authored in its vocabulary and information architecture, but visually remains close to a reusable consulting template. The strongest signature is the Match / Reconcile / Explain sequence. The page needs one tangible artifact—an anonymized exception queue, reconciliation view or before/after data example—to make the service feel earned rather than merely described.

#### What's Working

- Outcome-led hero with a clear “Best when” qualification panel.
- Logical problem → decisions → deliverables → CTA narrative.
- Strong semantic structure, responsive grid collapse, focus styles and tokenized palette.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Add proof, not just deliverable labels.** Show one representative reconciliation artifact. Suggested command: `$impeccable delight`.
- **[P1] Replace or reframe the unrelated article proof point.** The scheduled-capacity article is not immediately legible as reconciliation evidence. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P1] Clarify the CTA promise.** Explain what the 30-minute call covers and what the visitor should bring. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P2] Reduce repeated text blocks.** Compress “Tools and system boundaries” to make space for proof. Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`.
- **[P2] Differentiate the service family.** Give reconciliation a signature visual module distinct from the other data-service pages. Suggested command: `$impeccable bolder`.

#### Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan / first-timer:** “Tolerances,” “denominator” and “system boundaries” need plain-language examples.
- **Riley / stress tester:** Duplicate records, missing IDs, currencies and late data are named but not demonstrated.
- **Casey / mobile:** The page is long; a mid-page or sticky mobile CTA could reduce scroll friction.

#### Questions to Consider

- What is the smallest real reconciliation artifact you can safely show?
- Is the discovery call the intended primary conversion, or should the page first offer a diagnostic/sample download?
- Which audience should recognize itself fastest: finance/control teams, commercial operations or data/analytics teams?
