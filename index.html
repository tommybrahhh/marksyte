Find the real growth block

Problem: strategy is too broad Project based

Sharpen the marketing or proposition strategy

Optimize the marketing strategy, product proposition or brand plan when the team has too many options and not enough clarity on what to bet on.

  • Where-to-play, audience, occasion or channel choices
  • Product positioning, claims and value proposition
  • Brand activation priorities and commercial story
