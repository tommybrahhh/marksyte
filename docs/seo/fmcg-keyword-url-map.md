# FMCG keyword-to-URL audit

Audit date: 2026-08-16  
Scope: English `/blog/` articles that directly support Marksyte’s FMCG, retailer, distributor, data integration, data quality, reconciliation or managed-data services

## Scope and method

The repository contains 30 English operational/data guides in scope. Travel-retail news, airport analysis, tourism articles and general AI/editorial posts were excluded unless the article’s primary job was directly connected to the FMCG data-service proposition. Spanish counterparts were not counted as additional English URLs.

The audit used the live HTML files under `blog/<slug>/index.html` as the source of truth. For each page, the review covered the document title, H1, description metadata, opening framing, H2/H3 spine, FAQ summaries, current service links, current related-article links and apparent search intent. No search volume, rankings, traffic or backlink claims are made because no such data is present in the repository.

## Recommended internal-link structure

Use a directional cluster model:

1. Diagnostic pages link to the relevant procedural pillar. For example, the retailer-ERP cause page links to the retailer-to-ERP reconciliation guide; the cutoff page links to the calendar bridge; and the SKU-failure page links to the SKU/EAN/GTIN mapping guide.
2. Procedural pages link to one adjacent specialist guide and one relevant service page. Avoid linking every page to every other page.
3. Pillars link down to their supporting pages with descriptive anchors and link back to the relevant Marksyte service page near the point where the operational problem becomes a service decision.
4. Distributor and dataset pages should route to `/services/data-mapping-integration/`, `/services/data-standardization/` and `/services/managed-data-analysis/` according to whether the reader needs mapping, intake controls or recurring operation.
5. Retailer-to-ERP, sell-in/sell-out, calendar, returns and exception pages should route primarily to `/services/data-reconciliation/` and secondarily to the illustrative case at `/study-cases/data-reconciliation-fmcg/`.
6. Product mapping and sell-out mapping pages should route primarily to `/services/data-mapping-integration/`, with `/services/data-standardization/` for pack, unit and file-quality issues.
7. The Excel workflow should be the manual entry point, the operating-model page the process-design hub, and the automation page the BOFU decision route into managed data operations.
8. Metrics pages should link back to the reliable sell-out dataset and data-standardization pages so that market share and sell-through are presented as outputs of a controlled dataset, not isolated calculations.
