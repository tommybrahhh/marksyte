## How to read this matrix

- **High**: credible fit with the new operational-data proposition and a candidate for the first Phase 6 reframe batch.
- **Medium**: useful editorial support with a genuine data, planning or operational angle, but not necessarily a service article.
- **Low**: relevant sector intelligence, but weak or artificial fit with the new service layer. Preserve the URL and keep editorial unless later evidence supports a different treatment.
- **Treatment** records the intended editorial direction. The Phase 6 reframing batch is now complete for the remaining promotional sections.
- **Related service** is assigned only where the connection is natural. An em dash means the article should remain independent editorial content.
