The decision

A broad range is not automatically a strong portfolio

Travel Retail adds unusual constraints: limited space, international shoppers, channel-specific pack roles, customer economics and a high cost of complexity. Domestic-market logic rarely transfers cleanly

We create an evidence-based role for each part of the portfolio and make the trade-offs explicit, so marketing, sales and category teams can work from the same decision model
